## Full description
Kharcha helps you understand where your money goes — in under 10 seconds a day.

• Add expenses, income and savings with a built-in calculator (type 120+45×2)
• See what's SAFE TO SPEND today based on your budgets or income
• Monthly budgets by category with early warnings
• Savings goals with a "save ₹X/month" nudge and progress tracking
• Recurring transactions for rent, salary, SIPs and subscriptions
• Split bills with friends and track who has paid you back
• Beautiful insights: category breakdown, daily spending, month-vs-month comparison, spending streaks
• Search, calendar view, CSV export, dark mode
• Your data syncs securely across devices. No ads. No bank access needed. Delete your account any time.
